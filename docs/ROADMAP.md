# Roadmap

## MVP

- High-conversion onboarding and interactive value demo
- Hard RevenueCat paywall
- Today lesson loop
- Audio-first spaced practice
- Phrase-oriented Words library
- Profile and learning preferences
- Southern and Northern female-voice TTS
- Secure Supabase progress foundation

## Post-MVP validation

- Content-authoring pipeline and native-speaker QA workflow
- Downloadable audio packs and cache controls
- Better speech feedback with explicit privacy consent
- Sign in with Apple account upgrade and cross-device sync
- Remote content manifest, rollout controls, and paywall experiments

## Bounded conversation

Add only after core retention demonstrates that learners complete lessons and
reviews. Scenarios remain task-specific, always end in two minutes or less, show
time remaining, and produce a recap that feeds the existing Words scheduler.
Mistral may generate bounded branch suggestions and structured feedback, but a
deterministic scenario controller owns timing, allowed intents, and safety.

## Expansion

- French customer-facing localization after English product validation
- More regional listening exposure without claiming accent uniformity
- Family/heritage learning paths
- Relocation and work scenario packs
- iPad layout refinement
