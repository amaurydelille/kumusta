Kumusta

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy explains what the Kumusta iOS app (“Kumusta”, “we”) collects, why, and the choices you have. Kumusta is published by Amaury Delille. Contact: amaurydelille92500@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), and the first name you give us.
  • Learning profile and progress: your goal, level and daily time, completed lessons, saved words, reviews, streaks and XP.
  • Leaderboard: your first name, avatar and XP are shown to other learners on the leaderboard.
  • Voice: when you start a speaking exercise or a live conversation, your microphone audio is sent to our speech-recognition provider to turn it into text. We do not store your recordings. For live conversations we store the text transcript of the conversation.
  • Subscription status: Apple processes payments. We receive whether your subscription is active and when it renews or ends, through our subscription provider. We never see your card details.
  • App usage: basic events about how the app is used (for example, a lesson finished), linked to your account, to fix problems and improve lessons.

How we use it

Only to run Kumusta: to keep your account and progress, grade your speaking, power conversations with Tuko, show the leaderboard, check your subscription, send the reminders you turn on, and improve the app. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that process it for us:

  • Supabase: hosting, database and sign-in.
  • Soniox, and Apple speech recognition as a fallback: turning your speech into text.
  • Anthropic: generating Tuko’s replies from your conversation text.
  • ElevenLabs: turning lesson text and Tuko’s replies into speech.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: subscriptions and payments.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Recordings are not kept. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile in the app, export your data, and delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account, which permanently deletes your account and learning data. You can turn off microphone access and reminders in your iPhone settings. You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data.

Children

Kumusta is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date.
