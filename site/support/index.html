Subscriptions

Kumusta subscriptions are billed by Apple. To cancel, change your plan or ask for a refund, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or visit reportaproblem.apple.com. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap Restore Purchases on the plans screen.

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and learning data. You can also email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in your Apple settings.
