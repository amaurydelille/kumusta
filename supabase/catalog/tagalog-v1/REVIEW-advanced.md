# Advanced Tagalog lessons: native-review notes

Points a native Metro Manila speaker should double-check, per lesson.

## 1. Meeting your partner's parents

- "nag-iipon na rin po kami": enclitic order (na rin po vs na po rin).
- "gusto muna naming maging handa talaga": natural, or would people say "gusto muna naming maging
  handa"?
- Addressing a partner's parents as Tito/Tita at a first dinner (vs Ma'am/Sir or Mommy/Daddy) is the
  default we assumed.
- "Next time, ako na po ang magluluto": Taglish switch natural with elders?

## 2. Chismis with your best friend

- "Marites" as a gossip label: still current, or already dated?
- "Bes" as a gender-neutral address; "chika" vs "tsismis/chismis" register.
- "dinelete" (vs "ni-delete" / "binura") spelling and naturalness.
- "patay ako" as playful "I'm dead": fine for the app's tone?

## 3. The OFW debate

- "sa abroad" (vs "sa ibang bansa") with an elder.
- "manatili" may sound a bit formal; "dito na lang" might be more natural.
- "malaki ang naisakripisyo ninyo": natural, or "malaki ang sinakripisyo ninyo"?

## 4. Everyday expressions

- Slang currency: "sana all", "charot", "ikaw na", "diskarte".
- "Sakay na lang tayo ng kahit anong bus": natural imperative, or "Sumakay na lang tayo"?
- "bago mag-alas dose" for "before twelve (midnight)".
- "Sayang 'yung ticket natin": singular "ticket" for two tickets is how we assume people say it.

## 5. Lola's adobo

- "turuan n'yo naman po ako": enclitic order and tone with a grandparent.
- Whether marinating first is the family-typical method; the "don't stir after adding vinegar" lore
  and "hilaw ang lasa".
- "Nilalagyan n'yo po ba ng konting asukal": natural, or "Naglalagay po ba kayo ng..."?
- "iba pa rin talaga ang luto ni Lola" said to Lola's face (third person) should read as
  affectionate.

## 6. Teleserye talk and videoke

- "hindi ko inakalang si Lorenzo pala ang tunay na ama": natural spoken form?
- "kayo na lang sa drinks" (elliptical) and "bibirit sa chorus".
- "Videoke tayo sa Sabado sa bahay": "sa bahay" for "at my place".

## 7. Office Taglish meeting

- Overall Taglish density: does it sound like a real Ortigas/BGC meeting or overdone?
- "mag-a-update" (future reduplication on an English verb) spelling.
- "medyo kulang po ang isang linggo para ma-test namin nang maayos" with a manager.

## 8. Forgot the monthsary

- "Wala akong maidadahilan": natural, or "Wala akong excuse" / "Wala akong dahilan"?
- "Bati na tayo, ha?" and "Miss na miss na kita" as the coaxing lines.
- "nagtatampo" / "magtampo" used of a partner of any gender.

## 9. Lolo's story

- "huwag mong kalilimutan" vs the more spoken "kakalimutan".
- "mga sulat lang ng lola mo ang nagpapalakas ng loob ko": natural for an older Batangas speaker (no
  Batangueño features were added on purpose).
- "Iyak nang iyak ang lola mo sa airport": the 1970s setting says "airport"; would Lolo say "sa
  airport" or "sa paliparan"?

## 10. Wedding toast

- "kakaisip kung paano siya lalapit": colloquial "kakaisip" (vs "kaiisip").
- "walang perpektong pagsasama, pero may pagmamahal na pinipili araw-araw": natural or too written?
- "Kaya itaas na natin ang ating mga baso": "ating" vs "mga baso natin"; is "Mabuhay ang bagong
  kasal!" the toast people actually shout?
